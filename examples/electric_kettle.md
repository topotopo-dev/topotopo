# Electric kettle

A modern electric kettle for a game or a product render: a clean upright body with a softly rounded base, a pouring lip
and a handle that grow out of it without a seam, a lid with a small knob and a base plate.

## In
- height: 0.18–0.32 m, default 0.24. Height of the whole kettle, knob included.
- belly: 0.12–0.2 m, default 0.15. Width of the body at its widest.
- spout_length: 0.01–0.06 m, default 0.035. How far the spout reaches out past the body.
- handle_thickness: 0.016–0.04 m, default 0.026. Thickness of the handle where you grip it.
- gap: 0.0008–0.003 m, default 0.0015. How far the lid stands proud of the top, which makes the edge line around it.

## Out
- kettle (mesh): the kettle in four named pieces, the body, the lid, the knob and the base plate, in matte white and
  brushed steel.

## Promises
- budget: the kettle stays under 6,000 triangles.
- closed: every piece is a closed solid with its faces pointing out.
- standing: the base plate sits on the ground, its underside at height 0.

## Look
A clean white kettle with an upright, slightly tapering body and crisp edges at the base and around the top. A short
pouring lip grows out of the top front in one smooth transition and the handle arches from the top down to the lower
back, thickest at the grip. A round lid rests on the flat top, standing slightly proud with a softly rounded edge, with a small steel knob, on a
flat steel base plate.
