# Hover bike

A sleek sci-fi hover bike for a game: one flowing teardrop body with a pointed, slightly drooping nose and a raised tail, two thruster pods under it that grow out of the body with glowing round outlets facing down, a seat cushion, low handlebars on a short stem and a small curved windscreen.

## In
- bike_length: 1.8–2.8 m, default 2.3. Length from nose to tail.
- width: 0.65–1.0 m, default 0.8. Widest point of the body.
- pod_size: 0.7–1.3, default 1. Size of the two thruster pods (1 = standard).
- nose_droop: 0–0.15 m, default 0.06. How far the tip of the nose hangs low.
- tail_rise: 0–0.25 m, default 0.1. How far the tail lifts.
- line_crease: 0–3, default 2. Sharpness of the two character lines along the flanks (0 = soft).
- hover_height: 0.05–0.5 m, default 0.15. Height of the lowest point of the bike above the ground.

## Out
- bike (mesh): the hover bike, with an off-white upper body, a graphite lower body, glowing cyan outlets, a dark seat and a tinted windscreen.

## Promises
- closed: every piece of the bike is closed, with every face pointing out.
- hovering: the lowest point of the bike is exactly hover_height above the ground.
- budget: under 10,000 triangles.

## Look
A long, smooth teardrop in off-white with a crisp character line from the nose along each flank, above a darker graphite underside that swells into two round pods; cyan outlets glow under the pods, a dark seat sits in the top, and a small tinted screen and low bars stand ahead of it.
