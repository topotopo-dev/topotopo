TopoTopo computes tangent space (glTF TANGENT export, bake_normal, the PBR preview) with the MikkTSpace WebAssembly
module that the three.js package ships as examples/jsm/libs/mikktspace.module.js (three.js itself is MIT, listed
above). That module is mikktspace-wasm by Don McCurdy (https://github.com/donmccurdy/mikktspace-wasm, MIT), a
WebAssembly build of the Rust crate mikktspace (https://github.com/gltf-rs/mikktspace, MIT OR Apache-2.0), which is a
